# Days in Lieu — clickable prototype

Our Lady's Grammar School, Newry. A demonstration of the Days in Lieu request app: teaching staff request days in lieu, the Principal approves or declines each day.

**This is a prototype, not the live system.** Everything runs in the browser with sample data; nothing is saved and no email is sent. The bar at the top switches who you are looking at it as (a teacher, the Principal, a first-time visitor). Reloading the page starts the demo afresh.

Open it: https://dgaj-g.github.io/days-in-lieu-prototype/
